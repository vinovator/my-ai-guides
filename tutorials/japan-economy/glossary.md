# Glossary

*Every term the guide uses, defined in a sentence and linked to the lesson that explains it.*

---

## A to F

| Term | Meaning | Taught in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Abenomics** | Shinzo Abe's 2012 programme of three "arrows": aggressive monetary easing, flexible fiscal policy and structural reform | [Lesson 4](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-04-abenomics-and-the-bazooka) |
| **Balance sheet recession** | A slump in which firms and households prioritise paying down debt, so even very low interest rates fail to revive borrowing | [Lesson 2](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-02-bubble-bust-and-deflation) |
| **Bid-to-cover ratio** | In a bond auction, the amount bid divided by the amount sold; a low ratio signals weak demand | [Lesson 11](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-11-reading-the-headlines) |
| **BOJ** | The Bank of Japan, the central bank, which sets short-term interest rates and carries out currency interventions decided by the Ministry of Finance | [Lesson 1](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-01-the-machine) |
| **Call rate** | The overnight interest rate at which banks lend to each other; the BOJ's main policy target | [Lesson 1](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-01-the-machine) |
| **Carry trade** | Borrowing in a low-interest currency, often the yen, to invest in higher-yielding assets elsewhere | [Lessons 3](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-03-false-dawn-and-the-super-yen) and [10](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-10-beyond-japan) |
| **Consumption tax** | Japan's national sales tax: 3% from 1989, 5% from 1997, 8% from 2014 and 10% from 2019, with 8% on food | [Lesson 4](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-04-abenomics-and-the-bazooka) |
| **Core CPI and core-core CPI** | Consumer prices excluding fresh food; and excluding both fresh food and energy | [Lesson 1](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-01-the-machine) |
| **Current account** | A country's trade in goods and services plus income flows with the rest of the world | [Lesson 9](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-09-the-big-debates) |
| **Deflation** | A sustained fall in the general level of prices | [Lesson 1](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-01-the-machine) |
| **Digital deficit** | The gap between what Japan pays foreign firms for cloud, software, advertising and content and what it earns from such services | [Lesson 9](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-09-the-big-debates) |
| **Duration** | A bond's sensitivity to interest rates: roughly the percentage price change for a one-point move in its yield | [Lesson 5](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-05-yield-curve-control) |
| **Fiscal dominance** | When a central bank's decisions are driven by the need to keep government borrowing affordable | [Lesson 9](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-09-the-big-debates) |
| **Fixed-rate purchase operation** | The BOJ's offer to buy unlimited amounts of a bond at a set yield, used to defend yield curve control | [Lesson 5](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-05-yield-curve-control) |
| **Forward guidance** | A central bank's public promises about the future path of interest rates | [Lesson 2](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-02-bubble-bust-and-deflation) |

## G to N

| Term | Meaning | Taught in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **"Good" and "bad" inflation** | Inflation driven by wages and domestic demand, versus inflation driven by a weak yen and costly imports | [Lesson 1](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-01-the-machine) |
| **GPIF** | The Government Pension Investment Fund, the world's largest pension fund | [Lesson 4](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-04-abenomics-and-the-bazooka) |
| **Hedging (currency)** | Fixing today the rate at which future foreign-currency income will be converted back, at a cost roughly equal to the gap in short-term interest rates | [Lesson 10](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-10-beyond-japan) |
| **Intervention** | Buying or selling currency to move its price; in Japan decided by the Ministry of Finance and carried out by the BOJ | [Lessons 6](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-06-the-yen-breaks) and [7](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-07-politics-war-and-a-nervous-bond-market) |
| **JGB** | Japanese government bond | [Lesson 1](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-01-the-machine) |
| **Monetary base** | Banknotes in circulation plus the reserves banks hold at the central bank | [Lesson 4](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-04-abenomics-and-the-bazooka) |
| **Mrs Watanabe** | Nickname for Japanese households trading foreign currencies | [Lesson 3](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-03-false-dawn-and-the-super-yen) |
| **Negative interest rates** | Charging banks for part of the reserves they hold at the central bank; −0.1% in Japan from 2016 to 2024 | [Lesson 4](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-04-abenomics-and-the-bazooka) |
| **Net international investment position** | What a country's residents own abroad minus what foreigners own in that country | [Lesson 10](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-10-beyond-japan) |
| **Neutral rate** | The interest rate that neither stimulates nor restrains the economy; estimated at about 1% to 2.5% for Japan | [Lesson 1](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-01-the-machine) |
| **NISA** | Japan's tax-free personal investment scheme, greatly expanded in 2024 | [Lessons 6](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-06-the-yen-breaks) and [9](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-09-the-big-debates) |
| **Nominal versus real** | Measured in today's money, versus adjusted for inflation | [Lesson 1](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-01-the-machine) |
| **Non-regular workers** | Part-time, temporary and contract employees, who are paid and protected less than permanent staff | [Lesson 2](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-02-bubble-bust-and-deflation) |

## O to Z

| Term | Meaning | Taught in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Overshooting commitment** | The BOJ's 2016 pledge to keep easing until inflation exceeded 2% and stayed above it stably | [Lesson 5](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-05-yield-curve-control) |
| **Phillips curve** | The tendency for low unemployment to push up wages and prices; very flat in Japan for two decades | [Lesson 5](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-05-yield-curve-control) |
| **Plaza Accord** | The 1985 agreement by five large economies to push the dollar down, which roughly doubled the yen | [Lesson 2](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-02-bubble-bust-and-deflation) |
| **Potential growth** | The pace an economy can grow without overheating; about 0.5% a year for Japan | [Lesson 1](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-01-the-machine) |
| **Primary deficit** | A budget deficit before interest payments | [Lesson 1](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-01-the-machine) |
| **Primary income** | Income from investments and work abroad: dividends, interest, profits and wages | [Lesson 9](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-09-the-big-debates) |
| **QE and QQE** | Creating money to buy bonds; QQE, Kuroda's 2013 version, also bought longer-dated bonds and stock funds | [Lessons 2](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-02-bubble-bust-and-deflation) and [4](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-04-abenomics-and-the-bazooka) |
| **Rate check** | Officials asking banks for exchange-rate quotes, a signal that intervention may be close | [Lesson 11](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-11-reading-the-headlines) |
| **Real effective exchange rate** | A currency's value against all trading partners, adjusted for differences in inflation | [Lesson 9](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-09-the-big-debates) |
| **Real interest rate** | The nominal interest rate minus expected inflation | [Lesson 1](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-01-the-machine) |
| **Safe haven** | An asset that tends to rise in a crisis; the yen, because Japanese money comes home | [Lesson 1](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-01-the-machine) |
| **Shunto** | The annual spring wage negotiations between large companies and unions | [Lesson 6](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-06-the-yen-breaks) |
| **Tail (auction)** | The gap between the average and the lowest accepted price at a bond auction; a long tail signals weak demand | [Lesson 11](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-11-reading-the-headlines) |
| **Tankan** | The BOJ's quarterly survey of business conditions | [Lesson 11](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-11-reading-the-headlines) |
| **Term premium** | The extra yield investors demand to lock up money for longer, which rises with fiscal and inflation risk | [Lesson 1](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-01-the-machine) |
| **Terms of trade** | The price of a country's exports relative to its imports; it worsens when import costs such as oil jump | [Lesson 7](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-07-politics-war-and-a-nervous-bond-market) |
| **Widowmaker trade** | Betting that JGB yields would rise, a trade that ruined many who tried it before 2022 | [Lesson 1](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-01-the-machine) |
| **Yield curve control (YCC)** | Targeting a specific long-term bond yield, as the BOJ did with the 10-year JGB from 2016 to 2024 | [Lesson 5](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-05-yield-curve-control) |
| **Zero interest rate policy (ZIRP)** | Holding the overnight rate at effectively zero, first adopted by the BOJ in 1999 | [Lesson 2](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-02-bubble-bust-and-deflation) |
| **Zombie firm** | An insolvent company kept alive by cheap credit | [Lesson 2](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-02-bubble-bust-and-deflation) |
