## 2024 to 2026: normalisation

| Date | Event | Why it mattered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 18 Sep 2026 | BOJ raises its rate to 1.25% (7 to 2 vote) | Highest since 1995; the yen still weakens because the Fed hiked two days earlier ([Lesson 7](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-07-politics-war-and-a-nervous-bond-market)) |
| 17 Sep 2026 | Takaichi reshuffles her cabinet | Pledges to keep prioritising growth |
| 16 Sep 2026 | Fed raises its rate to 3.75% to 4% | First US hike since 2023 keeps the rate gap wide |
| 15 Sep 2026 | Cabinet approves the outline of the food tax cut | Food rate to fall from 8% to 1% from April 2027 to March 2029 |
| 4 Sep 2026 | Fiscal 2027 budget requests reach a record ¥143 trillion | Adds to fiscal worries at the long end |
| 1 Sep 2026 | 10-year JGB yield reaches 3% | First time since 1996; the 30-year yield sets a record |
| 30 to 31 Jul 2026 | Heavy yen buying by Japan, then a joint US-Japan intervention | First joint action since 2011, after the yen neared 164 per dollar |
| 30 Jul 2026 | Takaichi announces the food consumption tax will fall to 1% for two years | First cut since the tax was introduced in 1989 |
| 25 Jun 2026 | Nikkei closes at a record 72,366 | An AI-led rally |
| 16 Jun 2026 | BOJ raises its rate to 1% | First time at 1% in 31 years; cuts to bond buying to stop from April 2027 |
| 30 Apr to 6 May 2026 | Japan intervenes to buy yen, about ¥11.7 trillion | First of 2026's record interventions |
| 28 Feb 2026 | US and Israel strike Iran; Strait of Hormuz largely closed | Energy shock for a country that imports almost all its oil |
| 8 Feb 2026 | LDP wins 316 of 465 seats | First single-party two-thirds majority in post-war history |
| Jan 2026 | Snap election called with a food tax pledge | Long-term yields surge on fiscal fears |
| 19 Dec 2025 | BOJ raises its rate to 0.75% | Then the highest since 1995 |
| Dec 2025 | ¥18.3 trillion supplementary budget | Largest outside the Covid years, mostly financed by new bonds |
| 21 Oct 2025 | Sanae Takaichi becomes prime minister | Japan's first female leader; "responsible and proactive" spending |
| Jul 2025 | US-Japan trade deal: 15% tariff and a $550 billion investment pledge | Caps the tariff threat for exporters |
| May 2025 | 30- and 40-year JGB yields hit records after weak auctions; rice prices double | First sign of bond-market resistance; the agriculture minister resigns |
| 24 Jan 2025 | BOJ raises its rate to 0.5% | Highest since 2008 |
| Oct 2024 | Ishiba's coalition loses its lower-house majority | Minority government tilts policy towards household relief |
| 5 Aug 2024 | Nikkei falls 12.4% | Carry trades unwind after a BOJ hike and weak US jobs data ([Lesson 6](?slug=japan-economy&lesson=lesson-06-the-yen-breaks)) |
| 31 Jul 2024 | BOJ raises its rate to 0.25% and plans to cut bond buying | Quantitative tightening begins |
| Jul 2024 | Yen weakens to about 162 per dollar | Weakest since 1986; about ¥15.3 trillion of intervention that year |
| 19 Mar 2024 | BOJ ends negative rates and yield curve control | First hike in 17 years |
| 22 Feb 2024 | Nikkei closes above its 1989 peak | A 34-year wait ends |
| Jan 2024 | Expanded NISA tax-free savings scheme launches | Households start moving cash into shares, many of them foreign |
