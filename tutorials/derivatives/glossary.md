## A to D

| Term | Meaning | Taught in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **American-style option** | An option that can be exercised at any time up to expiry | [Lesson 4](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-04-options-paying-for-uncertainty) |
| **Arbitrage** | Buying something where it is cheap and selling the equivalent where it is dear, locking in a riskless profit; it keeps related prices in line | [Lesson 3](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-03-the-price-nobody-forecasts) |
| **At the money** | An option whose strike is about equal to the current price of the underlying | [Lesson 4](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-04-options-paying-for-uncertainty) |
| **Auto-deleveraging** | On some crypto exchanges, automatically reducing profitable positions to cover losses that liquidations could not | [Lesson 13](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-13-when-the-plumbing-became-the-story) |
| **Backwardation** | A futures curve in which later contracts are cheaper than nearer ones, usually because the commodity is scarce today | [Lesson 3](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-03-the-price-nobody-forecasts) |
| **Basis** | The gap between a futures price and the spot price of the underlying; it shrinks to zero at expiry | [Lesson 3](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-03-the-price-nobody-forecasts) |
| **Basis risk** | The risk that a hedge and the exposure it hedges move by different amounts | [Lesson 6](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-06-futures-and-daily-settlement) |
| **Basis trade** | Buying government bonds, usually with borrowed money, and selling the matching futures to capture a small price gap | [Lesson 6](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-06-futures-and-daily-settlement) |
| **Binary option** | An option that pays a fixed sum or nothing, depending on whether a condition is met | [Lesson 2](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-02-the-four-building-blocks) |
| **Black-Scholes formula** | The 1973 formula that prices an option from six inputs by building a copy of it | [Lesson 3](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-03-the-price-nobody-forecasts) |
| **Breakeven** | The price at expiry at which an option position neither makes nor loses money | [Lesson 4](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-04-options-paying-for-uncertainty) |
| **Call option** | The right, but not the obligation, to buy at the strike price | [Lesson 2](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-02-the-four-building-blocks) |
| **Cap (interest rate)** | A series of options that pays a borrower whenever a floating rate rises above a ceiling | [Lesson 7](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-07-swaps) |
| **Cash settlement** | Settling a contract by paying the price difference rather than delivering the underlying | [Lesson 6](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-06-futures-and-daily-settlement) |
| **CFD (contract for difference)** | A cash-settled contract with a trading firm on the price change of an asset | [Lesson 10](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-10-derivatives-for-retail-investors) |
| **Cheapest to deliver** | The bond a seller of government bond futures will choose to deliver, from a basket of eligible bonds | [Lesson 6](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-06-futures-and-daily-settlement) |
| **Clearing house (central counterparty, CCP)** | The body that becomes buyer to every seller and seller to every buyer, collects margin and absorbs defaults | [Lesson 8](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-08-the-plumbing) |
| **Close-out netting** | Collapsing all trades with a defaulted counterparty into a single net amount owed one way | [Lesson 8](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-08-the-plumbing) |
| **Collar** | Buying a put and paying for it by selling a call, which boxes an investment's outcome between a floor and a ceiling | [Lesson 5](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-05-the-greeks-hedging-and-strategies) |
| **Compression** | Tearing up chains of offsetting trades, which shrinks notional amounts without changing anyone's risk | [Lesson 1](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-01-what-a-derivative-is) |
| **Contango** | A futures curve in which later contracts cost more than nearer ones, reflecting the cost of financing and storing the commodity | [Lesson 3](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-03-the-price-nobody-forecasts) |
| **Convertible bond** | A bond with a call option on the issuer's shares attached | [Lesson 2](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-02-the-four-building-blocks) |
| **Cost of carry** | The cost of holding an asset until a future date: interest, plus storage, minus any income it pays | [Lesson 3](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-03-the-price-nobody-forecasts) |
| **Counterparty risk** | The risk that the other side of a contract cannot pay what it owes | [Lesson 9](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-09-how-things-blow-up) |
| **Covered call** | Owning shares and selling call options on them, giving up gains above the strike in exchange for the premium | [Lesson 5](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-05-the-greeks-hedging-and-strategies) |
| **Covered interest parity** | The arbitrage that sets a currency forward rate from the spot rate and the two interest rates | [Lesson 3](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-03-the-price-nobody-forecasts) |
| **Credit default swap (CDS)** | A contract that pays out if a borrower defaults, in exchange for regular premiums | [Lesson 7](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-07-swaps) |
| **Credit Support Annex (CSA)** | The part of an ISDA agreement that sets the collateral rules between two firms | [Lesson 8](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-08-the-plumbing) |
| **Credit triangle** | The rule of thumb that a CDS spread is roughly the default probability times the loss given default | [Lesson 7](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-07-swaps) |
| **Cross-currency swap** | Exchanging principal and interest in two currencies, so that debt in one currency becomes debt in another | [Lesson 7](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-07-swaps) |
| **CVA (credit valuation adjustment)** | The part of a derivative's price that covers the risk of the counterparty defaulting | [Lesson 14](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-14-derivatives-at-work) |
| **Default fund** | A pool contributed by a clearing house's members to absorb losses beyond a defaulter's margin | [Lesson 8](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-08-the-plumbing) |
| **Default waterfall** | The order in which a clearing house uses resources to absorb a member's default | [Lesson 8](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-08-the-plumbing) |
| **Delta** | How much an option's price moves for a one-unit move in the underlying; also the hedge ratio | [Lesson 5](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-05-the-greeks-hedging-and-strategies) |
| **Delta hedging** | Holding delta times the options in the underlying, and adjusting as delta changes, to neutralise the direction | [Lesson 5](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-05-the-greeks-hedging-and-strategies) |
| **Derivative** | A contract whose value depends on the price of something else, the underlying | [Lesson 1](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-01-what-a-derivative-is) |
| **DV01** | The change in a position's value for a one-basis-point move in interest rates | [Lesson 7](?slug=derivatives&lesson=lesson-07-swaps) |
