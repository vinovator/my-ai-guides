# Glossary

*Every term the guide uses, defined in a sentence and linked to the lesson that explains it.*

---

## A to D

| Term | Meaning | Taught in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Arbitrage** | Buying where something is cheap and selling where it is dear, across place, time or quality, which pulls related prices into line | [Lesson 4](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-04-hedging-speculation-and-arbitrage) |
| **Backwardation** | A forward curve in which prompt delivery costs more than later delivery; a sign of tight supply | [Lesson 6](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-06-the-forward-curve) |
| **Basis** | The gap between a local physical price and the futures price | [Lesson 4](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-04-hedging-speculation-and-arbitrage) |
| **Benchmark** | A reference price used to price many contracts, such as Brent, WTI or the LBMA Gold Price | [Lesson 7](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-07-the-outside-forces) |
| **Bill of lading** | The document issued when goods are shipped; it acts as a receipt and as title to the cargo | [Lesson 12](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-12-commodities-at-work) |
| **Buffer stock** | A stockpile bought and sold by an agency to hold a price within a range | [Lesson 10](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-10-shocks-that-wrote-the-rules) |
| **CBAM** | The EU's Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism, a charge on the carbon embedded in certain imports | [Lesson 12](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-12-commodities-at-work) |
| **Cash and carry** | Buying a commodity now, storing it and selling futures, when the futures price more than covers storage and financing | [Lesson 6](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-06-the-forward-curve) |
| **Cash settlement** | Settling a contract by paying the price difference instead of delivering the commodity | [Lesson 3](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-03-physical-barrels-paper-contracts) |
| **Clearing house (central counterparty)** | The body that becomes buyer to every seller and seller to every buyer, guaranteeing trades | [Lesson 3](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-03-physical-barrels-paper-contracts) |
| **Commitments of Traders** | A weekly US report showing how producers, swap dealers and funds are positioned in futures | [Lesson 7](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-07-the-outside-forces) |
| **Commodity** | A raw or lightly processed material standardised enough to trade without inspecting each unit | [Lesson 1](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-01-what-makes-a-commodity) |
| **Commodity currency** | A currency that tends to strengthen when the country's main exports become more valuable, such as the Australian dollar | [Lesson 8](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-08-what-commodity-prices-move) |
| **Contango** | A forward curve in which later delivery costs more than prompt delivery; a sign of ample supply | [Lesson 6](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-06-the-forward-curve) |
| **Convenience yield** | The benefit of physically holding a commodity now, such as keeping a refinery running | [Lesson 6](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-06-the-forward-curve) |
| **Convergence** | The closing of the gap between futures and physical prices as a contract approaches expiry | [Lesson 3](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-03-physical-barrels-paper-contracts) |
| **Corner and squeeze** | Controlling so much deliverable supply, or being so short of it, that prices spike as positions are forced closed | [Lesson 10](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-10-shocks-that-wrote-the-rules) |
| **Cost of carry** | The storage, insurance and financing cost of holding a commodity over time | [Lesson 6](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-06-the-forward-curve) |
| **Crack spread** | A refiner's margin: the value of the fuels produced minus the cost of the crude | [Lesson 6](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-06-the-forward-curve) |
| **Crush spread** | An oilseed processor's margin: the value of soymeal and soy oil minus the cost of the soybeans | [Lesson 6](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-06-the-forward-curve) |
| **Dated Brent** | The assessed price of physical North Sea crude cargoes loading soon; the anchor of global oil pricing | [Lesson 7](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-07-the-outside-forces) |
| **Demand destruction** | A lasting fall in consumption caused by high prices, as buyers economise or switch | [Lesson 5](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-05-supply-demand-and-the-shock-absorber) |

## E to M

| Term | Meaning | Taught in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Elasticity** | How strongly buyers or sellers respond to a change in price; commodities are inelastic in the short run | [Lesson 5](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-05-supply-demand-and-the-shock-absorber) |
| **Exchange for physical (EFP)** | Swapping a futures position for physical commodity; its price signals stress between paper and physical markets | [Lesson 11](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-11-the-new-fault-lines) |
| **Export ban** | A government restriction keeping a commodity at home, which tightens the world market for everyone else | [Lesson 7](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-07-the-outside-forces) |
| **Forward** | A private agreement to trade a set quantity at a fixed price on a future date | [Lesson 3](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-03-physical-barrels-paper-contracts) |
| **Forward curve** | The line of futures prices for successive delivery months | [Lesson 6](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-06-the-forward-curve) |
| **Fungibility** | The property of being interchangeable: one unit is as good as another | [Lesson 1](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-01-what-makes-a-commodity) |
| **Futures** | A standardised forward traded on an exchange, cleared and margined daily | [Lesson 3](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-03-physical-barrels-paper-contracts) |
| **Good Delivery** | The London specification a gold or silver bar must meet to be accepted by the whole market | [Lesson 1](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-01-what-makes-a-commodity) |
| **Hedging (long and short)** | Taking a derivatives position that offsets the price risk of a physical exposure; buyers hedge long, sellers hedge short | [Lesson 4](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-04-hedging-speculation-and-arbitrage) |
| **Initial margin** | The deposit posted to open a futures position | [Lesson 3](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-03-physical-barrels-paper-contracts) |
| **Letter of credit** | A bank's promise to pay an exporter once it presents documents that meet agreed terms | [Lesson 12](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-12-commodities-at-work) |
| **Leverage** | Controlling a large position with a small deposit, which magnifies gains and losses | [Lesson 3](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-03-physical-barrels-paper-contracts) |
| **Liquidity risk** | The danger of being unable to meet cash calls, such as margin, even when positions are sound | [Lesson 4](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-04-hedging-speculation-and-arbitrage) |
| **Lot size** | The quantity covered by one contract, such as 1,000 barrels on NYMEX or one kilogram of gold on MCX | [Lesson 3](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-03-physical-barrels-paper-contracts) |
| **Marginal cost of supply** | The cost of the most expensive producer still needed to meet demand, towards which long-run prices gravitate | [Lesson 5](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-05-supply-demand-and-the-shock-absorber) |
| **Mark-to-market** | Revaluing positions daily at current prices, with gains and losses settled in cash | [Lesson 3](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-03-physical-barrels-paper-contracts) |
| **Merit order** | The ranking of power stations by running cost; the last one needed sets the electricity price | [Lesson 12](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-12-commodities-at-work) |
| **Minimum Support Price (MSP)** | The price at which Indian government agencies stand ready to buy specified crops | [Lesson 9](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-09-the-us-the-uk-and-india) |

## O to Z

| Term | Meaning | Taught in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Open interest** | The number of futures or options contracts still open | [Lesson 3](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-03-physical-barrels-paper-contracts) |
| **OPEC+** | OPEC members plus non-OPEC producers led by Russia, coordinating output since 2016 | [Lesson 10](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-10-shocks-that-wrote-the-rules) |
| **Option (call or put)** | The right, not the obligation, to buy (call) or sell (put) at a set strike price, in exchange for a premium | [Lesson 3](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-03-physical-barrels-paper-contracts) |
| **Over the counter (OTC)** | Trades negotiated privately rather than on an exchange | [Lesson 3](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-03-physical-barrels-paper-contracts) |
| **Position limit** | The maximum position a trader may hold, set by a regulator or an exchange | [Lesson 9](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-09-the-us-the-uk-and-india) |
| **Price reporting agency** | A firm such as Platts or Argus that assesses prices in physical markets | [Lesson 7](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-07-the-outside-forces) |
| **Roll and roll yield** | Replacing an expiring contract with a later one, and the gain or loss that results | [Lesson 6](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-06-the-forward-curve) |
| **Spare capacity** | Production that can be switched on within weeks; the oil market's emergency buffer | [Lesson 7](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-07-the-outside-forces) |
| **Spark spread** | A gas-fired power station's margin: the price of electricity minus the cost of the gas to make it | [Lesson 6](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-06-the-forward-curve) |
| **Speculator** | A trader who takes on price risk for the chance of a profit, supplying liquidity to hedgers | [Lesson 4](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-04-hedging-speculation-and-arbitrage) |
| **Spot price** | The price for immediate or near-term delivery | [Lesson 3](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-03-physical-barrels-paper-contracts) |
| **Spread** | The difference between two related prices across time, location, quality or processing | [Lesson 6](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-06-the-forward-curve) |
| **Stagflation** | High inflation and weak growth at the same time, the typical result of a supply shock | [Lesson 8](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-08-what-commodity-prices-move) |
| **Stocks-to-use ratio** | Stocks at the end of a season divided by a year's consumption; a gauge of how tight a crop market is | [Lesson 5](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-05-supply-demand-and-the-shock-absorber) |
| **Strategic reserve** | Emergency stocks of oil (or grain) held by governments | [Lesson 9](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-09-the-us-the-uk-and-india) |
| **Supercycle** | A long upswing in commodity prices, lasting a decade or more, driven by structural demand | [Lesson 7](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-07-the-outside-forces) |
| **Swap** | An exchange of a floating price for a fixed price over a period, settled in cash | [Lesson 3](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-03-physical-barrels-paper-contracts) |
| **Tender period** | In India, the days before expiry when holders of deliverable contracts declare their delivery intentions | [Lesson 9](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-09-the-us-the-uk-and-india) |
| **Terms of trade** | The price of a country's exports relative to the price of its imports | [Lesson 8](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-08-what-commodity-prices-move) |
| **Troy ounce** | The unit for precious metals: 31.1035 grams, about 10% heavier than an ordinary ounce | [Lesson 1](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-01-what-makes-a-commodity) |
| **Variation margin** | The daily cash payment of gains and losses on futures | [Lesson 3](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-03-physical-barrels-paper-contracts) |
| **Warehouse receipt or warrant** | A document of title to stored commodity; LME warrants represent metal in approved warehouses | [Lesson 3](?slug=commodity-markets&lesson=lesson-03-physical-barrels-paper-contracts) |
