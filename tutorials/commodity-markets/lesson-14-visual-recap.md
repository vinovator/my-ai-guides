## That is the whole guide

You started with a tonne of copper that nobody needed to inspect. You now know who trades it and why, how futures, margin and hedges work, why prices are so violent in the short run and so cyclical in the long run, what the forward curve is saying, how a price travels into inflation, interest rates and household bills, how three very different countries live with it, and what it looks like when it breaks.

The last thing worth saying is the one from Lesson 13. Before you believe anyone's forecast, look at the market's own evidence: the curve, the stocks and the gap between physical and paper prices. It is free, it is updated every day, and it has no story to sell.
